How an order moves

Every order follows the same five stages. You get a named merchandiser and a shared timeline from day one.

01

Tech pack review

We check construction, measurements and fabric spec, then return a costing and open questions.

3–5 days
02

Sampling

Proto, fit and pre-production samples from our in-house sample room. Fabric sourced from approved mills.

2–3 weeks
03

Bulk production

Allocated to the partner factory best suited to the product. Weekly progress photos and line reports.

30–45 days
04

Inspection

Inline and final inspection to AQL 1.0 or 2.5. Third-party inspection welcome.

2–3 days
05

Packing & shipping

Packed to your carton and labelling spec. FOB Hai Phong or door-to-door via forwarder.

Sea 25–35 days to US West Coast
